About Us

Our Genesis & Evolution

Thirty plus years of cricket with passion, unity, community service and social impact — from a coastal neighbourhood to a regional powerhouse.

1994

The Beginning

Founded in the vibrant coastal town of Surathkal by a group of passionate cricketers under the leadership of our inaugural captain, Shafiulla. What started as a neighbourhood dream in Kana quickly grew into one of Dakshina Kannada's most respected cricket clubs.

Our Core Principles

Built on three unwavering pillars that have guided every decision, every match, and every community initiative since our founding day.

Disciplined Excellence
Uncompromising Integrity
Passion for Cricket

Evolution into a Powerhouse

What began as a neighbourhood team has evolved into a dominant force in underarm cricket. For over a decade we fielded players exclusively from Kana; as the sport grew in Mangalore, so did we — strategically welcoming talent from across the region. Today, strengthened by our partnership with Trescon, Torpedos Kana stands as a testament to sporting excellence and strategic evolution.

A Beacon of Unity

Established during challenging times in the 1990s, Torpedos Kana chose to stand for inclusiveness above all else. We built a sporting environment where talent and character were the only measures — welcoming players regardless of religion, caste, or social boundary. This commitment to brotherhood remains deeply woven into our club's fabric to this day.

Community Impact

Making a positive difference through sports, education support, and community service programs across Dakshina Kannada.

Brotherhood & Unity

Promoting local culture and welcoming every player across all backgrounds — talent and character are our only measures.

"At Torpedos Kana, we're not just building a team, we're building a community. We're dedicated to fostering a spirit of camaraderie, discipline, and passion for the game. As President, I'm committed to supporting our players, support staff and members in their pursuit of excellence, both on and off the field."

"We're also committed to making a positive impact in our community. We're proud to have provided the ground floor of our club building, free of cost, to run an Anganwadi school. It's heartening to see over 20 young students benefiting from this initiative, and we're honored to play a small part in shaping their futures."

Mohammed Saleem

President, Torpedos Kana

Legacy

One of our proudest achievements stands as a testament to the vision of our beloved late leader, S Mohammed Suratkal (Liberty). The public gymnasium we built was his dream — a space where Kana's youth could gather to build not just their physical strength, but also forge bonds of unity and fellowship.

Pioneering Player Welfare

Torpedos Kana became the first club to implement a structured monthly salary system for players, with compensation tied to performance grades — revolutionizing the landscape of underarm cricket and setting new standards for player welfare.

Empowering Potential

In proud partnership with the Trescon Foundation, we are committed to breaking the cycle of educational inequity — ensuring students from marginalized and backward communities have the financial backing and professional mentorship they need to succeed.

Scholarships & Beyond

Through this collaborative effort, we provide a robust safety net for students who possess merit and drive but lack the financial resources to pursue higher education.

Comprehensive Scholarships

Jointly funded tuition support covering books and learning materials for school and college.

Direct Fee Assistance

A streamlined process to help students facing immediate financial hurdles and prevent dropouts.

Community-Specific Outreach

Targeted support for students from underserved communities, promoting inclusivity in professional sectors.

Our partnership with Trescon Foundation allows us to double our impact, moving from mere financial aid to sustainable community empowerment.

Career Guidance & Support

The Torpedos Kana & Trescon Foundation alliance focuses on bridging the "guidance gap." We provide the networks and mentorship often missing for students from backward communities.

Joint Mentorship Programs

A wider network of professionals from both organizations providing real-world insights.

Personalized Career Roadmaps

Individualized counseling to identify strengths and navigate entrance exams or applications.

Employability Skill Workshops

Combined resources for soft skills, digital literacy and interview techniques.

Our Shared Philosophy

The collaboration between Torpedos Kana and the Trescon Foundation is built on the belief that collective action creates lasting change. We remove the barriers of cost and lack of information, allowing students to focus on what matters most: their growth and their future.
